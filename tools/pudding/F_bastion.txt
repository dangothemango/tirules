@26
R LRR 78.6a: "Before assigning hits, players may use their units' 'Sustain Damage' abilities to cancel hits." LRR 1.19: abilities in the same window are resolved "in initiative order, beginning with the active player." Apollo triggers "When one of your galvanized units is destroyed," so it resolves in that window, before the remaining hits are assigned.
